Music Theory, Made Accessible

AgaTage develops "MusicQuiz", an app designed to help you learn music theory intuitively. Instead of reading thick textbooks, you can deepen your musical knowledge effortlessly during your spare time.

Our Purpose

We started this project with a simple goal: to make learning music theory easier and more fun. Instead of forcing mechanical memorization, we aim to provide a user-friendly experience where you can naturally master the mechanics of music at your own pace.
