■ 5 Comprehensive Quiz Modes

◯ Chord Quiz (All Keys with Audio) Free

Learn the notes of each chord across all keys by solving quizzes with audio. Master chord construction using both your eyes and ears.

~ Included Chords ~

  • Major 7th Chords
  • Dominant 7th Chords
  • Minor 7th Chords
  • Half-Diminished (m7b5) Chords

Covering all keys for the above chords, you will fully master basic chord constructions.

◯ Key Quiz (Key Signature ID) Free

Train yourself to instantly identify major and minor keys by looking at the number of sharps (#) or flats (b) on the staff.

Supporting all keys, mastering this mode will allow you to identify the key of any sheet music at first glance.

◯ Interval Quiz (All Keys) Premium

Thoroughly conquer "intervals," the smallest unit of music, in 5 steps.
Develop the ability to instantly determine "which note corresponds to which interval" relative to the root note.

  • Beginner: M3 (Major 3rd), m3 (Minor 3rd), P5 (Perfect 5th)
  • Intermediate 1: M7 (Major 7th), m7 (Minor 7th)
  • Intermediate 2: 9th, 11th, 13th
  • Advanced 1: b9, #9 (Altered Tensions)
  • Advanced 2: #11, b13 (Altered Tensions)

From basic 3rds and 5ths to complex altered tensions, this mode covers it all.
By training this skill, you'll be able to instantly recognize intervals, which greatly accelerates your progress on instruments like piano or guitar.

◯ Scale Quiz (Ear Training) Premium

In addition to knowledge questions about scale characteristics and notes, this mode features "Ear Training Quizzes" where you identify scales by listening to the actual audio. (All keys supported)
Master the following scales through both theory and ear training.

~ Included Scales ~

  • Ionian (Major)
  • Dorian
  • Phrygian
  • Lydian
  • Mixolydian
  • Aeolian (Minor)
  • Locrian
  • Major Pentatonic
  • Minor Pentatonic
  • Blues Scale
  • Altered Scale
  • Diminished (Con Dim)
  • Harmonic Minor
  • Phrygian Dominant (HMP5B)
  • Melodic Minor
  • Whole Tone Scale
  • Lydian b7

◯ Progression Quiz (Practical) Premium

This mode consists of both theoretical knowledge questions (e.g., "What is I-V-vi-IV in the key of C?") and ear training quizzes where you listen to audio and identify the progression.

Featuring 10 major and 10 minor progressions across all keys, you can experience the actual sound of basic progressions alongside your theoretical knowledge.

By covering dominant motions and standard progressions, you will develop a highly practical musical ear.

■ Features to Support Your Learning

📖 Theory Cheat Sheets

Topics covered in the quizzes are summarized in handy cheat sheets with audio. Input knowledge through the cheat sheets and output it through the quizzes to dramatically boost your learning efficiency.

⚙️ Customizable Learning

Set quizzes to be asked in batches of "5 questions" or "10 questions" to make it easy for beginners. Also features volume adjustment for sound effects and a Dark Mode.

🌐 Bilingual Support

Fully supports switching between English and Japanese, syncing perfectly with your device's system language.
